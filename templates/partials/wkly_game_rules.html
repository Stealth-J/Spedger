New to the game?

Welcome to the game

If this is your first time playing, here’s a quick guide to help you get started and make the most out of each gameweek.

How to Play

  1. Enter your Sporty slip code and submit.
  2. Before submitting, review all the games on your slip carefully to ensure everything is accurate — once you submit for the week, it cannot be changed.
  3. All selections that are not supported are automatically removed from the slip.

Game Rules

  1. Your Slip must have a combined total odds of at least 10.0
  2. Each slip must contain at least 5 selections.
  3. Only include games that fall between {{ current_wkly_game.start_date|format_date_with_days }} to {{ current_wkly_game.end_date|format_date_with_days }} . This window represents the full gameweek
  4. You can only submit one slip per week

Leaderboards and Points

Throughout the gameweek, your slip will earn points based on your results. You can track your standing anytime on the leaderboards.

  1. Players who achieve a perfect record for their slip (100% correct selections) always take priority on the leaderboard.
  2. If more than one player finishes with 100%:
    • The slip with the higher total odds ranks higher
    • If total odds are identical, the player with the single highest odd among their selections gets the edge.
  3. Those without perfect slips would have their points determined by their success rate
  4. Success rate is calculated as: (Number of correct selections ÷ Total Selections) x 100
    • A higher success rate places you higher on the leaderboard, regardless of how many total selections were made.
    • When multiple players have the same success rate, total odds are used as the tiebreaker.
    • If total odds also match, the slip with the highest single odd selection wins the tie.
