Frequently asked Questions

Can you make money from this app?

No. You cannot make money directly from this app. Its services may aid you make money through other platforms though.

Is this a betting application?

No it's not. Because of how synonymous prediction is with betting, they might get mixed together but you do not need any stake or money to be wagered before predictions can be made

About this app

Spedger is a platform designed to encourage healthy, non-financial sports prediction. It allows users to enjoy the challenge of making predictions and competing with others, similar to a fantasy-style experience, but without any monetary risk. The platform also provides advanced insights based on past predictions to help users improve the accuracy of their future picks.
