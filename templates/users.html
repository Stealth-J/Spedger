Help

Starting a Duel

  1. You initiate a duel by challenging a friend and submitting your slip code.
  2. Once submitted, your code is final and cannot be edited or replaced.
  3. The challenged user must accept the duel and submit their own slip code to begin.
  4. As the challenger, you may define a minimum total odds requirement for your opponent’s slip. This ensures both parties compete on meaningful predictions and prevents ultra-low-risk entries.

Participation Rules

  1. Duels can only be initiated between friends.
  2. Any incoming duel requests can be viewed and managed from the Duels tab.
  3. You can also monitor active duels progress from the Duels tab.

Determining the Winner

  1. The duel is primarily decided by win percentage, measuring overall prediction accuracy.
  2. If both participants have the same win percentage, total odds are used as the tie-breaker.
  3. If total odds are also equal, the duel is declared a draw.

Visibility & Fair Play

  1. A participant’s slip code remains hidden from the opponent until the duel is fully concluded.
  2. This ensures fair competition and prevents strategy copying during active duels.

Duel Completion

  1. A duel concludes only after every game on both participants’ slips has been played.
  2. Once concluded, results are finalized and both codes become visible.
