Privacy Policy — Spedger

What we collect

Account info
Full name, username, password (hashed), nationality, reason for signing up, favourite team (optional), and privacy setting.
Prediction & usage data
Predictions you make, timestamps, and aggregated behaviour data used to provide analytics on prediction patterns.
Local storage
No personal data is kept in the browser localStorage.

How we use data

We use collected data to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Deliver prediction analytics
  • Personalize the experience
  • Secure the platform
  • And Improve features

Sharing & third parties

We do not sell or trade personal data. We may share minimal data if required by law or to protect our rights. We do not share prediction data with third parties except in aggregated and anonymized form.

Retention

We retain account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, personal data is removed and prediction logs are anonymized.

Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your personal data through your Profile. You can also update your personal information in the Settings section.

Security

We use standard measures to protect your data (password hashing, secure storage). No system is perfect — keep your password safe.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact us through Support.
